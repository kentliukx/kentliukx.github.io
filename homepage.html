Kaixuan Liu 刘凯暄

I am a third-year undergraduate in Mechanical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, enrolled in the Zhiyuan Honors Program. My interests lie in robot learning, contact-rich manipulation, and robotics systems.

I work with Prof. Cewu Lu and Prof. Chuan Wen in the Machine Vision and Intelligence Group on force-feedback teleoperation. Previously, I worked with Prof. Chenkun Qi on quadrupedal ladder climbing.

I expect to graduate in July 2028 and am seeking summer research opportunities in robotics, robot learning, and contact-rich manipulation.
